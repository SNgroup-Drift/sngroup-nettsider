Visningsrommet for Nobia

Here we show the whole solution behind a kitchen order at Studio Sigdal Innlandet, from drawing to fitted kitchen and invoice. Visningsrommet is our digital showroom. All data is made up.

The solution in brief

Sigdal CRM

The hub. Customers, quotes, orders, purchase orders, cases and fitting in one place. Everything else connects here.

Supplier connections

Sigdal through CET, appliances through Tradeplace and other suppliers by email, files or EDI. It all ends up in the same model in the CRM.

Hengsel

The fitter app. The fitting company receives the jobs, and the fitter has drawings, goods and quality checks on the phone.

My page

The customer portal. The customer sees the quote, contract, delivery week and documents, and approves right there.

Key points

1Entered once

The order is drawn in CET and arrives in the CRM as Nobia XML. Nobody types the same information twice.

2Deviations caught before production

The order confirmation is checked automatically against the drawing. The salesperson only sees what differs, while it can still be corrected.

3The customer does not need to call

Status, delivery week and documents are on My page, and delivery is announced by text message. The salesperson does not need to chase either.

4Suppliers on three levels

Level 1 for everyone: email and PDF with a fixed reference. Level 2: files and EHF e-invoices. Level 3: a direct connection through EDI, Tradeplace or API.
